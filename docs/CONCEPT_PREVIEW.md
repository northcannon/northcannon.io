# Change intelligence concept preview

The About-page customer workspace and mock evidence packet are fictional design
specimens. Their supplier, dates, decision count, and review state are illustrative;
they are not customer data, product behavior claims, evaluation results, or evidence.

The specimen is rendered only in the isolated review build. Its copy remains
pending in the public claim registry and must not appear in production output
without founder copy approval. The preview uses native links and disclosure controls,
with no browser JavaScript, forms, service connections, or executable decisions.
